⚠ The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at all 18 spots) is the list's only missable trophy: its first meditation is a session with Lucia in Chapter 1. Take the meditation the moment the game offers it in Chapter 1, then visit the other 17 spots in free roam. Every other trophy is earned in the story, replayable, or repeatable in free roam — see the roadmap below. Sources: Sixshore and GGRecon.
