Roadmap to the Platinum

PowerPyx rates the hunt 4/10 difficulty and about 200 hours in a single playthrough, with no online trophies and no New Game+. The story takes 60–80 hours; the 100% checklist takes about 100 more; clean-up takes 10–20. This atlas follows that shape.

  1. Phase 0 — Before you start 30 minutes of discipline

    Save manually at the start and end of every chapter. There is no mission replay, so a manual save is your only way back (PowerPyx). Never turn cheats on — cheats disable trophies and saving (PowerPyx). Avoid the Irredeemable criminal profile: it is permanent and makes police far more aggressive (GTASpotter).

    Covers: the save habit that protects every trophy below.

  2. Phase 1 — Play the story, do the one missable thing 60–80 h

    Finish all five chapters and the six named missions. While you play, scan vehicles and places with the WAiNK app, try all nine activity types, and work the stranger strands as they appear. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, take Lucia's balcony meditation at Jason's house — the one possibly missable trophy (PowerPyx, KeenGamer). Andy's strand is Lucia-only; Tyrone's and Bret's are Jason-only (GTASpotter).

    Covers: 12 story trophies, the 5 named stranger finales, Positive Mental Attitude.

  3. Phase 2 — The 100% checklist ≈100 h

    After the final mission, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion (PowerPyx). The checklist is lenient — roughly 60% of true completion — needing 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 card sets, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned and 100 of 255 animals studied. It still demands every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest in full. Work it top to bottom; free roam continues after the story (PushSquare).

    Covers: A Long Road, 250 stunts, 42 gold shooting-range medals, 14 drone races, 100 WAiNK locations, 5 bank vaults, 9 gangs, 52 areas, 8 garages.

  4. Phase 3 — Strangers, leftovers, Platinum 10–20 h

    Finish any stranger strands left, sweep the remaining activities and robberies, and launder the last marked bills. Every quest-giver tied to a trophy comes back after the story (GTASpotter), so nothing here is a race. When the last counter falls, Vice Got a Price! pops.

    Covers: Strangers & Freaks, the last challenges, Vice Got a Price!

Missable audit — what the published guides actually say

Audited against every one of the 55 official descriptions and the published guides. The honest result: one trophy is flagged as possibly missable, and seven more deserve a watch. Everything else is obtainable in a single playthrough — free roam continues after the final mission (PushSquare) and every quest-giver tied to a trophy returns (GTASpotter).

Save discipline (the real protection)

  • No mission replay. Save manually at the start and end of every chapter (PowerPyx).
  • Cheats disable trophies and saving (PowerPyx) — do not touch them until the Platinum pops.
  • Irredeemable profile is permanent — avoid it; police become far more aggressive (GTASpotter).
  • Hourglass quests expire, but they do not count for trophies (GTASpotter) — let them go without worry.
  • Character-locked strands: Andy is Lucia-only; Tyrone and Bret are Jason-only (GTASpotter).

Notes on the list

  • Official list: the 55 names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them; Exophase and PSNProfiles agree on all 55. Frozen 18 Nov 2026. One name carries asterisks as published: “T**s Ass Drug Money”.
  • Hidden trophies: 28 of the 55 are hidden on PlayStation until earned — the 12 story trophies, the 5 stranger finales, and 11 world and moment trophies. Spoiler protection hides exactly those 28 until you earn them or switch it off.
  • Unconfirmed methods: Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin have no confirmed unlock method in any published guide yet. This atlas says so plainly instead of inventing steps — check the sources again as the community pins them down.
  • Estimates are estimates: difficulty stars and hours are this atlas's own judgement, benchmarked against PowerPyx (~200 h, 4/10) and PushSquare (up to 300+ h). Your mileage will vary.
  • Counts: 250 stunts of 397 in the game, 42 shooting-range challenges, 14 drone-race locations, 18 meditation spots, 18 card sets, 80 challenges in 8 groups of 10, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 8 garages, 277 base-model vehicles, 255 animals.
