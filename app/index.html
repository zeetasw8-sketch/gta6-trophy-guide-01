The road to Vice Got a Price!

Four phases, in order. The story first, the stranger strands second, the open-world grind third — and 100% completion last, because A Long Road is the real Platinum gate.

Save discipline — the one rule that saves a playthrough

There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, plus one untouched save from just before the final mission (15 manual slots + 1 autosave). Never use cheats on your trophy save: they switch trophies off and stop saving. Everything outside the story stays available after the credits, and the 100% checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion only after the story — but work done earlier still counts.
